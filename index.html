Before you begin.

What is available in Phase 1?

Public-facing pages show real launch information. Operator modules remain design screens. The landing-page workspace is an illustrative preview, not an active sales console.

How do I access the system?

Use “Log in with ZENTRA-id” to continue to the configured ZENTRA Project entry point. Access to operator functions depends on the platform’s current rollout and your account permissions.

Does the same billing schedule apply to every project?

The designed scope includes HDA Third Schedule progressive billing for applicable projects and e-invoicing. Configuration must match the project type and contract; do not assume the same schedule applies to industrial developments.
